# Flare Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Flare does not collect, sell, or share personal information. It does not contain analytics, advertising, tracking, or a developer-operated account service.

## Data stored on your Mac

Flare stores job results, saved job boards, filters, preferences, and optional AI parsing data locally in the app's sandboxed container. This information remains on your Mac unless you explicitly export your saved job boards.

You can clear cached job results from Flare's settings. Removing Flare's container or uninstalling the app and its data removes the remaining local information.

## Network requests

Flare connects directly to public company career sites and applicant tracking systems to find job postings. Those sites receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address and request headers, and handle it under their own privacy policies.

If you enable AI parsing, Flare downloads an optional language model from Hugging Face. Parsing then runs locally on your Mac; careers-page content is not sent to an AI service.

Flare may open job links, the project support page, or this privacy policy in your default browser when you ask it to.

## Notifications and files

Flare requests notification permission to alert you about new jobs. Import and export access is limited to files you select through the macOS file picker.

## Contact

Questions and privacy requests can be submitted through the [Flare support page](https://github.com/dchernopolskii/Flare/issues).
